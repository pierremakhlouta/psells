Price drops

Listed items that have sat long enough to count as not selling, or since before PSells recorded listing dates, and whose price has room to come down. The break-even price is the partner's cut: sold at it, an item makes nothing and loses nothing. The room is the listed price less that; items with little room are left out. Most room first. Reviewed takes an item off the list, as a change of its price does, until it has gone as long again without selling.
